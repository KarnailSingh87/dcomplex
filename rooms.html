Frequently Asked Questions

Room rent in Hamirpur, amenities & booking policies

How much is room rent in Hamirpur at DC Complex? expand_more

Room rent ranges from ₹2,000/month for Single Room sets to ₹5,500/month for 2BHK family sets and balcony suites. There are zero broker commissions and security deposit is strictly 1 month refundable.

Are rooms available near Govt Polytechnic & Hamirpur Bus Stand? expand_more

Yes. DC Complex is situated along the main road near Baru Chowk, just 1.1 km (3 minutes) from Govt. Polytechnic Hamirpur and 2.4 km from the Hamirpur Bus Stand. Direct buses and auto rickshaws are available right outside.

What amenities are included with the rooms? expand_more

Every room comes with attached private washrooms, instant 24/7 water geysers, high-speed JioFiber Wi-Fi, independent submeters for electricity tariff, vehicle parking, in-house gym access (RFC Royal Fitness Club), and ground-floor grocery mart.

Can students and bachelors rent rooms here? expand_more

Yes, DC Complex welcomes college students from Polytechnic and NIT Hamirpur as well as working professionals. We offer single room sets (₹2,000/mo) and sharing sets with no rigid curfew locks.

How do I schedule a visit or book a room? expand_more

You can call the owner directly at +91 62300 13209 or click the WhatsApp button to schedule an in-person room inspection anytime between 8:00 AM and 8:30 PM.
